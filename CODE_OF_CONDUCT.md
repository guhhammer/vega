# Code of Conduct

## The short version

Be decent. Assume the person you are replying to is trying to build the same
thing you are.

## What is expected

- **Argue about the work, not the person.** "This leaks the sender to relays" is
  a good comment. "This is amateur" is not, and it is also less useful, because
  it does not say what to change.
- **Take correction well, and give it kindly.** Everyone here will be wrong
  about something; the point of review is to find it early.
- **Respect that people have different amounts of time.** Nobody owes anyone a
  fast reply, and unanswered does not mean ignored.
- **Assume good faith until it is clearly gone.**

## What is not acceptable

Harassment, personal attacks, discrimination on any grounds, publishing private
information, sexualised language or imagery, and deliberate derailing.

There is one more, specific to this project: **do not use this repository to
coordinate attacks on people**. Vega exists so that people can talk privately.
Using its issue tracker or discussions to plan harm to anyone — including
targeting the users of any other messenger — is grounds for an immediate ban.

## Scope

Anywhere this project happens: issues, pull requests, discussions, and any space
where you are representing Vega.

## Reporting

Email the maintainer. Reports are handled privately, and whoever reports is not
required to make the matter public.

Responses range from a private word, through editing or removing content, to a
temporary or permanent ban. The maintainer decides, explains the decision to the
person concerned, and does not litigate it in public.
